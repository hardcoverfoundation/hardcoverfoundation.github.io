# Design Feedback Prompts for Claude

## Too generic

The homepage still feels like a generic nonprofit template. Reduce the number
of equal-sized cards. Create stronger editorial hierarchy with one dominant
feature, numbered chapter labels, thin rules, deliberate asymmetry, and more
negative space. Remove unnecessary rounded corners and decorative gradients.

## Too close to the reference mockups

This composition is too close to the supplied moodboard. Replace the matching
structure with an original layout. Change the hero composition, section rhythm,
card mechanics, image treatment, and proportions while preserving only the
high-level goals of confidence, contrast, youth-centered storytelling, and
strong calls to action.

## Too dark

The page relies too heavily on dark surfaces. Keep the hero and one major
impact section dark, but move mission, approach, programs, stories, and news
onto warm paper or white surfaces. Create an editorial rhythm rather than one
continuous black page.

## Too many cards

Reduce the number of boxed cards. Use editorial lists, split sections,
full-width features, image-and-copy compositions, indexes, and structured
sequences. Every section does not need a container around each item.

## Logo overuse

Use the full logo in the header and footer. Use the emblem at most once as a
subtle watermark or chapter marker. Remove repeated logo treatments from
individual sections.

## Weak mobile layout

Review the page at 390px. Maintain at least 20px horizontal page padding,
prevent cropped headings, simplify competing typography, stack actions where
necessary, provide practical 44px touch targets, and ensure each section has
one clear focal point.

## Style without credibility

The page communicates style but not enough institutional credibility. Improve
clear pathways to programs, leadership, governance, reports, impact evidence,
partnerships, news, events, contact information, and tax-exempt status without
making the homepage excessively long.

## Expanded literacy is unclear

The design mentions expanded literacy but does not explain it quickly enough.
Clarify the progression from reading words to reading self, people, culture,
systems, and opportunity. Keep traditional book literacy important while
showing how Hardcover extends it into life.

## READ Framework feels decorative

The READ Framework currently looks like a four-letter graphic rather than a
methodology. Show movement from recognition to empowerment, articulation,
development, and impact. Give each phase meaningful explanatory content and a
clear relationship to programs.
